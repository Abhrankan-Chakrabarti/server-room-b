## EPILOGUE: Clean Exit
Abhrankan did not push the encrypted volume to a remote repository. He left the laptop open on the fused desk, its screen locked, the raw payload sitting on a physical, air-gapped drive that would never see a network connection again.

He walked out of Server Room B, passing the three-inch structural offset in the concrete without looking at it. The data center was quiet now, the fans whirring at their standard, analog frequencies. The universe had patched over the vulnerability by simply dropping the socket, leaving behind a handful of permanent rendering errors in Sector 4 as the only evidence that the base mesh had ever been modified.
